Personal Stylist in Reigate

Reigate is where my styling story started. For five years I ran The Secret Dress House bridal boutique here, and I still love working with women across Reigate and the surrounding area.

These days I'm an independent personal stylist, helping women who feel stuck with their style rediscover what works for them. Whether it's a wardrobe that no longer fits your life, an event coming up, or a wedding dress search that's starting to feel overwhelming, I can help.

Bridal styling from a former Reigate boutique owner

Between 2013 and 2018 I helped hundreds of brides find their dress at The Secret Dress House. I know how the bridal appointment system works, which questions to ask, and how to keep the process fun rather than stressful. Now I offer that experience as an independent bridal stylist: I come with you to your appointments, on your side and with no shop to sell for.

Everyday styling in Reigate

For everyday wardrobes, a Wardrobe Rescue at your home is often the best place to start: we edit what you own and build outfits from it. Follow that with Colour Analysis or a Personal Shopping trip and you'll know exactly what to buy, and what to walk past.
