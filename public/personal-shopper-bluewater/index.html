Bluewater · Kent

Personal Shopper at Bluewater

Make Bluewater work for you. A three-hour guided shopping trip at Bluewater in Greenhithe, Kent, with a stylist who knows how to find what suits you.

Bluewater has a huge choice under one roof, which is exactly why it can feel overwhelming. Most of my clients tell me the same thing: they go shopping, try on a dozen things, and come home with nothing, or with something they never wear. A personal shopping trip fixes that.

What to expect

  • A free 15-minute call first to talk about your lifestyle, the gaps in your wardrobe and your budget.
  • Three hours at Bluewater together. I'll pick the shops and styles that work for your shape and colouring, bring pieces to the fitting room, and show you how to wear them together.
  • No pressure. You only buy what you love and will actually wear.

Who it's for

Women whose lives have changed: a new job, a new body after children, a milestone birthday, or simply a wardrobe that doesn't feel like you any more. If you've lost your style identity, we'll find it again.
