Personal Shopper at Lakeside

A guided shopping trip at Lakeside Shopping Centre in West Thurrock, Essex: three hours, the right shops for your shape and budget, and no more buying mistakes.

Big shopping centres are brilliant when you know what you're looking for, and exhausting when you don't. That's where I come in. I'm Kelly Harvey-Welsh, a London College of Style trained personal stylist, and you may have seen me on Lakeside's social channels.

How a Lakeside shopping trip works

  • Before the day: a free 15-minute call to talk through your style, your budget and what you need.
  • On the day: three hours together at Lakeside. I'll steer you to the shops and pieces that suit you, from high street to premium, and take the guesswork out of the fitting room.
  • Afterwards: you leave with outfits that work together, not just a bag of separate buys.

Any budget works. We agree it before the trip, and my job is to make it go further.

Get more from your trip

Pair your shopping day with a Colour Analysis session first, so you know exactly which shades to look for, or a Wardrobe Rescue so we know what's already in your wardrobe and what's genuinely missing.
