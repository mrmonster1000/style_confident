Style help for women in Croydon and across south London who feel stuck with their wardrobe and want to feel like themselves again.

Maybe your life has changed: a new job, a new shape, children, a milestone birthday. Maybe you've just lost track of what suits you. I'm Kelly Harvey-Welsh, an independent personal stylist, and I help women in Croydon, Purley, Sanderstead and the surrounding area find their style again, with no fashion rules and no judgement.

Colour analysis: the best place to start

If you're not sure where to begin, start with Colour Analysis. In two hours we find the shades that make you look awake and well, and the ones that drain you. It's the quickest way to stop buying things you never wear, and it makes every shopping trip afterwards easier.

Wardrobe and shopping support

From there, a Wardrobe Rescue at home sorts what you've got into outfits that work, and a Personal Shopping trip fills the gaps. I work across every budget, from the high street to designer, and we agree it up front.

Can't fit in a home visit? An Online Style Session over video works from your own wardrobe, and I send personalised shopping links afterwards.
