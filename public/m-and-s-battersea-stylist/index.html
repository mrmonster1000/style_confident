M&S styling at Battersea Power Station

In-store styling at M&S Battersea is an M&S service, so appointments are arranged with the store, not through this website. Pop into M&S at Battersea Power Station and ask about personal styling. You can see the latest looks from the store on TikTok at @mandsbatterseafashion.

When to book Style Confident instead

Style Confident is my independent styling business. Book with me directly when you want help that goes beyond one shop:

  • A Wardrobe Rescue at home: editing what you already own and building new outfits from it
  • Personal Shopping across any brands, from high street to designer, in London, Surrey, Lakeside or Bluewater
  • Colour Analysis, so you know which shades suit you before you buy anything
  • Bridal and event styling, including TV appearances
  • Online sessions if you're not near London

Style Confident is Kelly Harvey-Welsh's independent business. It is not run by, affiliated with or endorsed by Marks & Spencer, and M&S in-store appointments can't be booked through this site.
