How styling in Guildford works

Most clients start with a free 15-minute call so I can hear what's not working. From there we usually do one of two things: a Wardrobe Rescue at your home, where we edit what you already own and build outfits from it, or a Personal Shopping trip in Guildford town centre to fill the gaps. Many clients book a Colour Analysis first, so every purchase after that is one that suits you.
