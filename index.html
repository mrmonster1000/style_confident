Questions?

Frequently Asked

Can a 'normal' person really use a personal stylist?

Absolutely! My services are designed for real women with real lives and budgets—not celebrities. If you've ever felt stuck with your style, I'm here for you.

What budget do I need for shopping appointments?

That's entirely up to you. We discuss budget before any shopping trip, and I work across all price points—from the high street to designer.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes! My Online Style Edit is perfect if you're not in London or Surrey. We work via video call and I send personalised shopping links directly to you.
